# Khazalite Holds — rules reference

This index is a structured companion to the New Recruit catalogue. The catalogue contains the selectable faction reference entry and unit rule notes.

## Faction battle traits

### Throgrim’s Beard

**Timing:** End of any turn; once per turn, army

If your general was destroyed this turn, give up to three friendly Khazalite Holds units one grudge mark each. Otherwise, choose up to three eligible triggers: a friendly unit was destroyed; a friendly Chromdrawn unit contests an objective also contested by an enemy; a friendly Grimdron unit charged, remains in combat, survived and did not destroy an enemy; or damage points were allocated to your general (mark a unit other than the general).

**Verification:** The Grimdron trigger wording was garbled in the captions.

### Reclaim the Carak

**Timing:** Passive

Immediately after an opponent gains control of an objective in your territory, give one grudge mark to a friendly Khazalite Holds unit. Each time a friendly unit retreats, after the retreat resolves remove one grudge mark from that unit if it has one.

### Let It Be Known

**Timing:** Your hero phase; once per turn

Pick a friendly Khazalite Holds hero, then a visible friendly non-hero Khazalite Holds unit within 12 inches. At the start of your next turn, that unit gains one additional grudge mark. Grudge marks are permanent and can stack.

### Gazul Remembered the Fallen

**Timing:** Passive

While at least half the models in a dwarf Khazalite Holds unit have been slain, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of its melee weapons. Models slain and later returned do not count as slain.

### Ancestral Vows

**Timing:** Once per battle per vow; any hero phase

There are five vows. Using one prevents you from using another ancestral vow in that battle round. See individual vow entries.

### We Honor Vala

**Timing:** Any hero phase

Pick up to three friendly dwarf Khazalite Holds units in combat. For each, pick an enemy unit in combat as its target. Roll a D3 for each friendly unit and its enemy target: the friendly unit heals that amount and the enemy suffers that many mortal damage. If your unit has the Vala keyword, use 3 instead of a D3. Healing can restore wounds or return models.

**Verification:** The full text was not read; this is the narrator’s summary.

### Engelhide, Our Light in the Darkness

**Timing:** Any hero phase

Pick up to one friendly non-hero Khazalite Holds infantry unit and up to one friendly Khazalite Holds infantry hero, both wholly within 6 inches of the same terrain feature and not in combat. Remove and set them up again wholly within 6 inches of a terrain feature and more than 9 inches from all enemy units. If two targets are chosen, they must be set up within 3 inches of each other.

**Verification:** Name is inconsistently rendered in captions.

### Grungni’s Stand Firm

**Timing:** Any hero phase

Pick up to three friendly dwarf Khazalite Holds units. Until the end of the battle round, while a target has not charged this battle round, subtract 1 from the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used for attacks that target it.

### Grimnir’s Wrath Will Burn Bright

**Timing:** Any hero phase

Pick up to three friendly dwarf Khazalite Holds units. Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to wound rolls for the targets’ combat attacks. A target that is a Grimdron infantry unit also has Strike-First if it charged in the same turn.

### Enlighten Us With Your Wisdom, Grumbrindle

**Timing:** Any hero phase

Until the end of the battle round, add 1 to casting rolls and chanting rolls for friendly Khazalite Holds units. Friendly units wholly within 12 inches of a friendly Khazalite Holds wizard or priest have a 4+ ward save against damage points inflicted by spell abilities, prayer abilities and abilities that use manifestations.

**Verification:** Captions were garbled; casting and chanting is the likely wording.

### Belligerent

**Timing:** Battle formation; passive

Subtract 3 from the control scores of enemy units while they are in combat with any friendly Khazalite Holds units.

**Verification:** Formation name was garbled in captions.

### Sworn Defenders

**Timing:** Battle formation; passive

Subtract 1 from hit rolls for shooting attacks that target friendly Khazalite Holds units while the target is wholly within friendly territory or contesting an objective you control.

**Verification:** Second condition was garbled in captions.

### Unyielding Anvil

**Timing:** Battle formation; passive

While an enemy unit is in combat with a friendly Khazalite Holds unit, subtract 1 inch from that enemy unit’s pile-in move, to a minimum of 1 inch.

### Wayfarer Nomads

**Timing:** Battle formation; once per turn, army reaction

When you declare a run or charge ability for a Khazalite Holds unit more than 6 inches from all other friendly units, a die roll determines how far it moves.

**Verification:** Full effect was not captured clearly; do not use as a fully implemented rule.

### Long of Beard

**Timing:** Heroic trait; passive

Each time a visible friendly Khazalite Holds unit wholly within 12 inches of this unit uses a Rally command, make three additional Rally rolls of a D6.

### Innate Resonance

**Timing:** Heroic trait; passive

If this unit is not a priest, it has Wizard 1. If it is already a wizard, it has Wizard 2 instead. Manifestations have Strike-Last while within 12 inches of this unit.

### Sworn to Vengeance

**Timing:** Heroic trait; end of any turn

If combat attacks made with this unit’s non-companion weapons allocate damage points to an enemy unit and that enemy unit is destroyed, give this unit a vengeance token (maximum 3) until the end of the next turn. Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit’s non-companion melee weapons for each token. The unit need not deliver the final blow.

### Hearth Flares

**Timing:** Artefact; your hero phase

Pick an objective within 12 inches of this unit. Until the start of your next turn, add 5 to the control score of friendly Khazalite Holds units while they contest that objective.

### Flame Scale Rune Cloak

**Timing:** Artefact; passive

Subtract 1 from hit rolls for attacks that target this unit if it has not charged this turn.

### Deep Earth Runic Weapon

**Timing:** Artefact; deployment phase

Pick one of this unit’s non-companion melee weapons as the runic weapon. Add 1 to its Rend for the battle. Then choose one: roll a die and on 3+ add 1 to its Attacks; or strike a rune, rolling a die and on 5+ add 1 to both its Attacks and Damage.

**Verification:** How often the die is rolled is unclear in captions.

### Runewright: Grudgement of the Forge

**Timing:** Passive

Add 1 to unbinding rolls, banishment rolls and Rhythmic Hammering rolls for this unit while it has any grudge marks.

### Runewright: Rhythmic Hammering

**Timing:** After casting a spell

Declare a spell ability from either Khazalite Holds spell lore and cast it normally. If the casting roll equals or exceeds the casting value, roll 2D6. If the result is lower than the spell’s casting value, the spell fails and its effect is not resolved. If the result equals or exceeds the casting value, increase the spell’s range by 3 inches. Repeat until a roll is lower than the casting value; range increases are cumulative.

**Verification:** Narrator says this does not work with spells that summon a manifestation.

### Rune of Dismay

**Timing:** Spell; casting value 6; unlimited

Until the start of your next turn, subtract 1 from the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons used by enemy units while within 6 inches of the caster.

### Rune of Courage

**Timing:** Spell; casting value not stated

Until the start of your next turn, re-roll charge rolls for friendly Khazalite Holds units wholly within 12 inches of the caster.

### Rune of the Furnace

**Timing:** Spell; casting value 8

Inflict D3 mortal damage on each visible enemy unit within 12 inches of the caster.

### Rune of Might

**Timing:** Spell; casting value 6; unlimited

Until the start of your next turn, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly Khazalite Holds units while within 3 inches of the caster.

### Rune of Sanctuary

**Timing:** Spell; casting value 7

Until the start of your next turn, friendly Khazalite Holds units wholly within 12 inches of the caster are not visible to enemy units more than 12 inches away from them.

### Rune of Warding

**Timing:** Spell; casting value 7

Until the start of your next turn, while wholly within 12 inches of the caster, attacks made by enemy units cannot score critical hits and are treated as regular hits.

### Recitation to Vala

**Timing:** Prayer; chanting value not stated; unlimited

Pick a friendly Khazalite Holds unit within 12 inches of the priest. On a chanting roll of 8+, pick a second eligible unit. Subtract 1 from wound rolls for attacks that target the unit until the start of your next turn.

### Recitation to Gazul

**Timing:** Prayer; chanting value not stated

Until the start of your next turn, enemy units and enemy manifestations cannot be set up within 12 inches of the chanting priest. On a chanting roll of 9+, use 18 inches instead.

### Recitation to Grimnir

**Timing:** Prayer; chanting value 5

Pick a visible enemy unit within 18 inches. Until the start of your next turn, each time it uses a core ability, after it resolves inflict 1 mortal damage on it. On a chanting roll of 10+, inflict D3 mortal damage instead.

## Important interpretation notes

- “KH” in the source means Khazalite Holds.
- “Chromdrawn” and “Grimdron” are retained as the transcript renders them.
- A grudge mark is the faction's token resource.
- The transcript paraphrases rules; wording here is not official battletome wording.
- Rules and profiles are included only to the extent supported by the transcript.
